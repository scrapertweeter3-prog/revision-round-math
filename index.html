The third revision round costs $3,435 at an agency

A fully loaded senior developer costs $219,480 a year once salary, benefits, and taxes land in the bill, which is $18,290 a month. Spread across the eighty hours that senior actually delivers in a month, the loaded hour runs $229. Hourly shops price rework by it. A revision round, sized at five hours of redo, is a $1,145 line item. Three rounds of back-and-forth on one feature and the rework bill alone reads $3,435.

The subscription prices the same work differently. Unlimited revisions until satisfied sit inside the flat $5,995 a month, so redo rounds stop generating invoices at all. At the $1,145 agency rate, five redo rounds run $5,725, within five percent of the subscription's entire month, and the sixth round crosses it. A client deep in changes has already beaten the hourly math before mid-month arrives.

The honest price is the calendar. One active request moves at a time, each card in for roughly seventy-two hours, so a month holds about ten cards. Five redo cards take fifteen of those thirty days. Dollars make the rework free; the slot still pays for it. The cheap move stays the same either way: put every objection in the first card, because feedback that lands before the build starts costs zero days.
